## Quy Trình Thực Hiện

### Bước 1: Solution Brainstorming

**Rules:**
- Quantity over quality
- No judgment during brainstorm
- Build on others' ideas
- Encourage wild ideas

**Inspiration Sources:**
- How do competitors solve this?
- How do other industries solve similar problems?
- What with unlimited resources?
- What's the simplest possible solution?
- What would AI/automation approach look like?

Generate 5-10 options.

### Bước 2: Evaluate Solutions

Score top 3 options:

| Criteria | Weight | Scoring Guide |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| Solves problem effectively | 30% | 1 = partially, 5 = completely |
| Fits appetite (time/budget) | 25% | 1 = way over, 5 = fits perfectly |
| User will understand/adopt | 20% | 1 = confusing, 5 = intuitive |
| Technical feasibility | 15% | 1 = very hard, 5 = straightforward |
| Scalable/Future-proof | 10% | 1 = dead end, 5 = extensible |

Chọn option có weighted score cao nhất.

### Bước 3: Shape the Solution

**"Shaped" nghĩa là:**
- **Rough enough** để team có room for creativity
- **Solved enough** để không có big unknowns
- **Bounded enough** để fits trong appetite

**Elements of a Shaped Solution:**

1. **Problem Recap** (1 sentence)
2. **Appetite**: ___ weeks, ___ people
3. **Solution Sketch**:
   - Key elements (boxes và arrows)
   - User flow: Entry → Steps → Outcome
4. **Rabbit Holes** (risks to avoid)
5. **No-Gos** (explicitly out of scope)
6. **Nice-to-Haves** (if time permits)

### Bước 4: Technology Decision (if applicable)

Cần quyết định:
- Build vs Buy?
- Tech stack?
- Integration approach?

Document rationale cho mỗi decision.

## Shaping Techniques

### Fat Marker Sketches
- UI rough sketch với nét to (không chi tiết pixel)
- Focus on arrangement, not visual design
- Shows what, not exactly how

### Breadboarding
- Components và connections (boxes & arrows)
- Shows flow, not appearance
- Identifies all the places và connections

### Scope Hammering
- Cut scope aggressively to fit appetite
- Ask: "What's the smallest version that works?"
- Nice-to-haves become future cycles

## Completion Checklist

```
□ Multiple solution options generated
□ Top options evaluated with consistent criteria
□ Best option selected with rationale
□ Solution shaped (not too vague, not too detailed)
□ User flow documented
□ Rabbit holes identified with mitigations
□ No-gos explicitly listed
□ Nice-to-haves captured for future
□ Technology decisions documented
□ Team aligned on shaped solution

GATE QUESTIONS:
□ Can this be built in the appetite?
□ Are the unknowns small enough to figure out during build?
□ Is the scope hammered down to essential?
□ Does the solution actually address the problem statement?
```
